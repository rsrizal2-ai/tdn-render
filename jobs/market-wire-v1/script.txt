The S and P five hundred climbed one point six percent, and the Nasdaq leapt two point three. Best week since April twenty twenty-five. Bitcoin slid under sixty thousand dollars, Ethereum dropped five percent, the worst of the majors, as money kept leaking out of Bitcoin funds. Here is the twist: the biggest whales quietly added one hundred twenty-one thousand Bitcoin this quarter. The big wallets are buying the dip.
