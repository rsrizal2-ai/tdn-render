Two hundred million baht a month. That is how much money police say was washed through two quiet properties in Pattaya, turned from cash into crypto.

On Friday evening, Thai police raided a house on Chaiyapruek Road, in Nong Prue. Inside, they say, five Chinese nationals were working exchange terminals, converting cash into USDT, the dollar stablecoin. Officers seized computers, thirteen mobile phones, routers, and three crypto hardware wallets.

At the same time, a second team hit a condo on the seventeenth floor of Lumpini Park Beach, in Jomtien. There they arrested a sixth man, who police describe as the operation's coordinator.

Police estimate the network moved around two hundred million baht every month, roughly six million US dollars. They say some of the money came from call-centre scams and online gambling, and that most of the alleged victims were in China.

Five of the six men held student visas, and were allegedly paid about fifty thousand baht a month. All six now face charges, including running an unlicensed digital-asset business, and working without work permits.

The raids were led by Police Region 2 commander Wasan Techa-akrakesom. And police say the investigation is still widening.
