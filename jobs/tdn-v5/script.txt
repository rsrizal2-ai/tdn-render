Taiwan's stock market just had the best day in its history. It lasted one day. On Tuesday, the Taiex rocketed three thousand three hundred ten points — up nine percent — the biggest one-day gain ever recorded. One company did the lifting: TSMC, forty percent of the whole market, pinned at its ten percent daily limit. Seoul partied too: the Kospi jumped six percent, back above five thousand. Then came Wednesday: down three thousand four hundred forty points in one morning. The record — erased in hours. The Daily Toon News — the world, in toons.
